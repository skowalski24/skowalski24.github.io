About the Game

Cult of Coin started as a simple idea: take the mindless joy of idle clickers and lean into it until it becomes a joke about itself.

You tap a coin to earn money. You spend that money on followers and generators so you do not have to tap as much. Eventually, you ascend, reset your progress, and do it again faster.

The game is free to play on Android with optional boosts: rewarded ads for things like doubling away-time coins, and a one-time remove-ads purchase for players who want the ritual without interruptions.

Play on Android

Cult of Coin is available now on Google Play. There are no current plans for an iOS release.
