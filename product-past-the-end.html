There is no traditional leveling grind. Progress comes from finding Power Tabs, equipment, spellbooks, and side-story rewards.

About the Game

Past the End is a narrative 2D Adventure RPG about what you can change in a world where not everything can be changed.

You play as Sam, a young woman traveling with her companions to find four elemental crystals and seal the Void, hoping to stop the Crimson Decay that is slowly consuming her mother. On the surface, it looks like a classic late-SNES / GBA-style adventure, but it is built around dense maps, authored encounters, and systems that reward curiosity over grinding.

The current release includes the full first chapter and the first half of Chapter 2 from a planned four-chapter story plus a post-game epilogue. The larger project is currently on pause, but this slice stands as a finished, tightly designed opening experience.
