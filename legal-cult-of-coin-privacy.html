Privacy Policy

Cult of Coin

Last updated: December 12, 2025

Made by Skows LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the mobile game Cult of Coin (the "App").

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Cult of Coin.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

We do not collect personal information directly.

Cult of Coin does not require account creation, login, or submission of personal details such as name, email address, or profile information.

All gameplay progress is stored locally on your device.

b. In-App Purchases

Cult of Coin offers optional in-app purchases, including:

  • A Remove Ads non-consumable purchase
  • Consumable purchases for in-game currency ("Faith")

All purchases are processed securely by Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

We do not collect or store payment information, credit card numbers, or billing details.

Purchase restoration is handled through the platform's built-in restore purchase functionality.

c. Advertising

Cult of Coin uses Google AdMob to display:

  • Banner advertisements
  • Rewarded video advertisements (e.g., to double offline earnings)

AdMob may collect limited device information such as:

  • Device identifiers
  • Approximate location (based on IP)
  • Ad interaction data

This information is used only to provide and improve advertising services.

When the device is offline, ad-based rewards are unavailable.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

d. Analytics

We do not currently use custom analytics or tracking tools to collect gameplay behavior or personal data.

If analytics are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

2. Data Storage

  • All gameplay data is stored locally on your device
  • No gameplay data is stored on external servers operated by us
  • You may delete your data at any time by:
    • Using an in-game reset option (if available)
    • Uninstalling the App

3. Children's Privacy

Cult of Coin is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in violation of this policy, please contact us so we can address the issue.

4. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request information about data handling practices
  • Request deletion of any locally stored data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising (via device or platform settings)

Because Cult of Coin does not store personal data on our servers, most requests can be fulfilled by adjusting device settings or uninstalling the App.

California residents may also have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

To exercise privacy-related requests, contact us at:
contact@madebyskows.com

5. Third-Party Services

Cult of Coin uses third-party services operated by:

  • Apple Inc.
  • Google LLC (Google Play Services, Google AdMob)

These services may collect information as governed by their own privacy policies.

We encourage you to review their policies for more information.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be posted within the App or on the App Store / Google Play listing.

Your continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact:

Made by Skows LLC
Email: contact@madebyskows.com
