Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Cult of Coin ("the Game") collects, uses, and protects your information. The Game is developed and operated by Made by Skows LLC.

Information We Collect

When you play Cult of Coin, we may collect:

  • Device identifiers (for save game synchronization)
  • Gameplay data (progress, achievements, statistics)
  • Purchase history (for in-app purchases)
  • Device information (device type, operating system)
  • Analytics data (app interactions, session length)

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Save and synchronize your game progress
  • Process in-app purchases
  • Improve game balance and features
  • Provide customer support
  • Analyze gameplay patterns to enhance the experience

In-App Purchases

Cult of Coin offers optional in-app purchases. Payments are processed through the App Store or Google Play. We do not directly handle payment information.

Advertising

Cult of Coin may display ads from third-party advertising networks. These networks may collect information to provide personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share anonymized, aggregated gameplay data for analytics purposes. Third-party services (analytics, advertising) may collect data as described in their privacy policies.

Data Retention

Gameplay data is retained as long as you have the game installed. You can reset your game progress or request data deletion by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Cult of Coin is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

Contact Us

For privacy-related questions about Cult of Coin, contact us through our contact page.
