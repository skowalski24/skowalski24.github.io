Data Deletion Request

Cult of Coin

Last updated: January 4, 2026

Made by Skows LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the mobile game Cult of Coin (the "App").

We respect your right to control your personal data. This page explains how to request deletion of any data associated with your use of Cult of Coin.

How Data Is Stored

Cult of Coin stores all gameplay data locally on your device. We do not collect or store personal information on external servers.

To delete your local game data, you can:

  • Uninstall the app from your device
  • Clear the app's data through your device settings

Third-Party Services

Cult of Coin uses the following third-party services that may collect and store limited data:

Google AdMob (Advertising)

AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data. To request deletion of data held by Google, please visit:

Google Account Data Deletion

Apple App Store / Google Play Store (Purchases)

If you made in-app purchases, transaction records are managed by Apple or Google. To request deletion of purchase data, please contact them directly:

  • Apple Support
  • Google Play Support

Note: Third-party services retain data according to their own privacy policies. We do not have access to or control over data stored by these services.

Submit a Data Deletion Request

If you believe we hold any data associated with your use of Cult of Coin and would like to request its deletion, please contact us:

Email: support@madebyskows.com

Please include "Cult of Coin Data Deletion Request" in your subject line and provide any relevant details to help us process your request.

We will respond to your request within 30 days.
