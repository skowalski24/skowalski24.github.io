Privacy Policy

Song of the Day

Last updated: December 03, 2025

Song of the Day ("the App") is a personal music diary app created and operated by Made by Skows LLC ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

By using Song of the Day, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

1.1 Account Information

  • Email address
  • Username
  • Profile picture (if you upload one)

1.2 User Content

  • Daily song posts (track, artist, album metadata)
  • Optional captions
  • Calendar of your posts
  • Likes and comments you make on other users' posts

1.3 Social & Interaction Data

  • Friends / connections inside the App
  • Notifications about likes, comments, and friend activity

1.4 Device & Technical Information

  • Device identifiers (such as a push notification token)
  • App version and basic technical data to help us fix bugs and improve performance

We do not collect passwords for third-party music services (like Apple Music or Spotify).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Display your posts, calendar, and profile to you and your friends
  • Send you push notifications about activity and updates (if you enable them)
  • Keep the App secure and prevent abuse
  • Understand how people use the App so we can improve it

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

Song of the Day uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) – authentication, database, storage, and analytics
  • Expo – app build and push notification delivery
  • Apple Music / iTunes Search APIs – song search and track metadata

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

  • We keep your account and content for as long as your account is active.
  • You may delete individual posts at any time from within the App.
  • You may delete your account from the App's settings. When you do, we will delete your profile and associated content within a reasonable time, subject to any data we are required to keep for legal or security reasons.

If you uninstall the App from your device, your account and data may still remain on our servers until you delete your account.

5. Children's Privacy

Song of the Day is intended for users 13 years and older.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us at the email below so we can remove the account.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data, such as:

  • The right to access the information we hold about you
  • The right to request correction or deletion of your data
  • The right to object to certain types of processing

You can exercise many of these rights directly in the App (for example, deleting your posts or your account).

For other requests, contact us at the email below.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and may notify you within the App.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at:

Email: contact@madebyskows.com
