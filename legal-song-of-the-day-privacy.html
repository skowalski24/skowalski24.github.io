Privacy Policy

Song of the Day

Last updated: December 12, 2025

Song of the Day (the "App") is a personal music diary application operated by Made by Skows LLC ("we," "us," or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the App. By using Song of the Day, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Username
  • Profile picture (if you choose to upload one)

1.2 User Content

We collect and store the content you create in the App, including:

  • Daily song entries (track, artist, and album metadata)
  • Optional captions or notes
  • Your personal post calendar
  • Likes and comments you make on other users' posts

This content is associated with your account and visible according to the App's social features.

1.3 Social & Interaction Data

We collect information related to your interactions within the App, such as:

  • Friends or connections
  • Likes, comments, and activity history
  • Notifications related to social activity (if enabled)

1.4 Device & Technical Information

We collect limited technical information necessary to operate the App, including:

  • Device identifiers such as push notification tokens
  • App version and basic diagnostic information

This information is used to deliver notifications, fix bugs, and improve performance.

1.5 Music Services

Song of the Day uses third-party music services (such as Apple Music or iTunes Search APIs) to search for songs and display track metadata.

We do not collect or store passwords or login credentials for third-party music services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Display your posts, calendar, and profile
  • Enable social features such as likes, comments, and connections
  • Send push notifications about activity and updates (if enabled)
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse
  • Improve the App's features and performance

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

Song of the Day uses third-party services to operate core functionality, including:

  • Firebase (Google) – authentication, database, storage, and analytics
  • Expo – app builds and push notification delivery
  • Apple Music / iTunes Search APIs – song search and metadata

These services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

4. Data Storage & Retention

Your account data and content are stored on secure servers provided by our service partners.

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active.

You may:

  • Delete individual posts at any time within the App
  • Delete your account from the App's settings

When you delete your account, your profile and associated content will be deleted within a reasonable time, subject to any data we are legally required to retain for security or compliance purposes.

Uninstalling the App does not automatically delete your account or server-stored data.

5. Children's Privacy

Song of the Day is intended for users 13 years of age and older.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us so we can remove the account and associated data.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data.

For users in the European Economic Area (GDPR):

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

For California residents (CCPA):

You have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal data)
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights

You can exercise many of these rights directly within the App, including deleting posts or deleting your account.

For additional privacy requests, contact us at:
contact@madebyskows.com

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information.

However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

If changes are made, we will update the "Last updated" date above and may notify you within the App where appropriate.
