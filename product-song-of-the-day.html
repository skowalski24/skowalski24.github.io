About the App

Song of the Day is designed to feel like a small daily ritual. You open the app, post your song, see what your friends chose, and move on with your day.

The app keeps social interaction lightweight. Reactions are a way to say "I heard this" or "I like this," not a scoreboard to chase. The main value comes from the daily choice and the archive it creates over time.

Over weeks and months, songs turn into a visual record of moods, seasons, and memories, with album artwork making each period easy to scan and revisit.
