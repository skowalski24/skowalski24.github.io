Data Deletion Request

Song of the Day

Last updated: January 4, 2026

Made by Skows LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the mobile app Song of the Day (the "App").

We respect your right to control your personal data. This page explains how to request deletion of any data associated with your use of Song of the Day.

How to Delete Your Account

Song of the Day allows users to permanently delete their account and associated data directly from within the app.

To delete your account:

  • Open the Song of the Day app
  • Go to Settings → Account
  • Select Delete Account and confirm

Once confirmed, your account and associated data are permanently deleted.

Data That Is Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is removed:

  • Your user account and profile
  • Your posts and shared song cards
  • Your friend connections
  • Any private song recommendations

Data Retention

Song of the Day does not retain personal data after account deletion, except where required by law. Any temporary backups are removed within 30 days.

Submit a Data Deletion Request

If you have trouble deleting your account from within the app, or believe we hold any other data associated with your use of Song of the Day, please contact us:

Email: support@madebyskows.com

Please include "Song of the Day Data Deletion Request" in your subject line and provide any relevant details to help us process your request.

We will respond to your request within 30 days.
