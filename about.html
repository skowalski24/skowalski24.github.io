About Me

I am Stephen "Skows" Kowalski, an independent designer and developer who creates games and mobile apps from concept through release.

What I Care About

I am interested in the space where systems, interaction, and human behavior meet: how a rule changes what someone chooses, how a constraint creates meaning, and how an experience can stay engaging without becoming overwhelming.

My work includes a shipped cross-platform social music app, a shipped Android idle game, narrative game development, and focused prototypes exploring progression, combat, resources, and player choice.

Games and apps are different forms, but I approach them with the same question: what should this experience help someone understand, feel, choose, or return to?

How I Work

These are the principles that show up whether I am tuning a game economy or shaping a mobile app flow.

Experience before complexity

A feature is only useful if people understand it and it improves the experience.

Behavior is part of the design

Rules, incentives, friction, and feedback all influence what players and users choose to do.

Constraints create direction

Limiting scope or available actions can make decisions clearer and more meaningful.

Iteration is the work

I enjoy building an idea, finding where it breaks down, gathering feedback, and refining it until it feels intuitive.

Shipping changes the questions

A public product introduces technical, platform, privacy, and support realities that static concepts never reveal.

Systems should serve people

I like systems that create interesting behavior without burying the person using them.
